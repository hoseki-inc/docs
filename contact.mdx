- **Email:** [support@hoseki.app](mailto:support@hoseki.app). Real people answer.
- **Bitcoin Control Center bugs and feature requests:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/hoseki-inc/bitcoin-control-center/issues)
- **Security issues:** report privately. See [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/hoseki-inc/bitcoin-control-center/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
- **A partner's request:** contact the partner first. Their support email is in the request email.

## What to include

- The product: Prove or Bitcoin Control Center.
- **Prove:** your account email, and the Claim ID if it's about a claim.
- **Control Center:** your platform (Mac, Windows, StartOS, or Umbrel) and app version.
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened.
- The exact error message, or a screenshot with balances and addresses hidden.

<Warning>Never share your seed phrase, private keys, API keys, xpubs, sign-in codes, or Control Center's data folder. Support will never ask.</Warning>

## Find your app version

This applies to Bitcoin Control Center. The version isn't shown inside the app yet.

| Platform | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Mac | In Finder, open **Applications**. Select **Bitcoin Control Center**. Choose **File** → **Get Info**. |
| Windows | **Settings** → **Apps** → **Installed apps**. Find **Bitcoin Control Center**. |
| StartOS | Open the Bitcoin Control Center service. The version is on its page. |
| Umbrel | Use the version of the bundle you installed from bitcoincontrolcenter.com. |
