A statement is a PDF of your balances for a date range. Sync all accounts first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Open **Statements** → **Generate Statement**.

    <Frame caption="Generate Statement.">
      <img
        src="/images/statements-generate.png"
        alt="Statements page with Generate Statement button"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose options">
    Pick accounts, dates, and a currency (USD, CAD, GBP, or EUR).

    <Frame caption="Accounts, dates, currency.">
      <img
        src="/images/statements-options.png"
        alt="Statement options: accounts, date range, and currency"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign">
    Sign the request on each hardware wallet when asked.

    <Frame caption="Sign on your device. Screenshot coming soon.">
      <img
        src="/images/statements-signing.png"
        alt="Hardware wallet signing prompt for proof of control"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Download the PDF.

    <Frame caption="Your finished statement. Screenshot coming soon.">
      <img
        src="/images/statements-download.png"
        alt="Finished statement ready to download"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Hoseki Verified

The badge means you signed a fresh message with each self-custody wallet. That proves you controlled it when you made the statement.

<Frame caption="The Hoseki Verified badge.">
  <img
    src="/images/statements-pdf-verified.png"
    alt="Statement PDF showing the Hoseki Verified badge"
  />
</Frame>

- It does not move funds.
- It does not apply to exchange, manual, or ETF holdings.

<Note>This is a Control Center statement. Prove statements are a different product. See [Control Center vs Prove](/getting-started/control-center-vs-prove).</Note>

On StartOS and Umbrel, Mempool must be running to create statements.
