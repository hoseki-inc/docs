A statement is a PDF of your self-custody Bitcoin for a date range. Think of it as a bank statement for your own wallets. It shows opening balance, money in, money out, and closing balance for each wallet.

## Before you start

- **Only verified wallets** can go on a statement. [Verify ownership](/bitcoin-control-center/accounts/verify-ownership) first.
- **Exchanges, manual, and ETF holdings are never included.** Ask your exchange for its own statement.
- **On StartOS and Umbrel,** Mempool must be running.

No verified wallets yet? The page says **First, Connect an Account**.

## Create a statement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Open **Statements** → **Generate Statement**. You can also start from **Home** or **Portfolio**.

    <Frame caption="Generate Statement.">
      <img
        src="/images/statements-generate.png"
        alt="Statements page with Generate Statement button"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick dates and currency">
    Choose a **Start Date**, **End Date**, and **Currency**: USD, CAD, GBP, or EUR.

    <Frame caption="Dates, currency, and wallets.">
      <img
        src="/images/statements-options.png"
        alt="Statement options: accounts, date range, and currency"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add contact details (optional)">
    Open **Contact Information**. **Name** comes from [Settings](/bitcoin-control-center/settings/app-settings). **Address** prints exactly as you type it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick wallets">
    All verified wallets are checked. Uncheck any you want to leave out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Select **Generate Statement**. It can take a few minutes. You can close the progress screen and come back from **Statements**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Choose **Full Period** or **Monthly** in the viewer. Select **Download**.

    <Frame caption="Your finished statement.">
      <img
        src="/images/statements-download.png"
        alt="Finished statement ready to download"
      />

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Date rules

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Earliest start: April 2023 | Price history for statements starts then. |
| Latest end: 2 days ago | So recent transactions have time to confirm. |
| Longest range: 2 years | Make two statements for longer periods. |

## Full Period or Monthly

**Download** saves the format you're viewing.

- **Full Period:** one summary for the whole range, then each wallet's transactions.
- **Monthly:** one summary per month, then that month's transactions.

## What's on the PDF

- A summary table: opening balance, money out, money in, and closing balance per wallet.
- A page of transactions for each wallet. Unconfirmed transactions show "PENDING".
- Your total in BTC. Non-USD statements also show an approximate value in that currency.
- Your name and address, if you added them.

Currency values use saved daily prices from Mempool. They are approximate.

## Hoseki Verified

Every statement carries the **Hoseki Verified** badge.

<Frame caption="The Hoseki Verified badge.">
  <img
    src="/images/statements-pdf-verified.png"
    alt="Statement PDF showing the Hoseki Verified badge"
  />
</Frame>

It means each wallet on the statement passed [proof of control](/bitcoin-control-center/accounts/verify-ownership) in Control Center: you signed a message with that wallet's key.

- Balances and transactions come straight from the Bitcoin blockchain.
- Signing does not move funds.
- Control was proven when you verified the wallet, not on the statement date.

## Manage statements

Open **Statements**. Switch between card and list view.

- **View** opens a statement. **Download** saves it.
- The trash icon deletes it, after you confirm. This can't be undone. Statement numbers are not reused.

## Where lookups go (desktop)

To build a statement, Control Center looks up every transaction in your wallets. By default this uses **mempool.space**. It is fast, but mempool.space sees your IP address and transactions.

To keep lookups on your own server, open **Settings** → **Statement data** → **Configured Electrum server**. It is slower, especially over Tor. See [Settings](/bitcoin-control-center/settings/app-settings#statement-data-desktop).

## If a statement fails

| Message | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| **Generation was interrupted.** | The app restarted during generation. Generate it again. |
| **Couldn't get Bitcoin prices for this statement.** | Check your internet connection. On StartOS or Umbrel, check Mempool is running. |
| **Couldn't load all transactions.** | Check your Electrum connection or statement data source. Try again. |
| **Statement date range cannot exceed 2 years** | Pick a shorter range. |
| **Statements must end at least 2 days ago** | Move the end date back. |
