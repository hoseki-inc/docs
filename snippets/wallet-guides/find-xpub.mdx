Your **extended public key** (xpub, ypub, or zpub) lets {appName} see your wallet's balance without being able to spend. Copy it from your wallet app, then paste it into {appName}.

<Tip>A `zpub` or `ypub` already says which address type you use, so you don't need to pick one. With an `xpub`, you also pick the script type.</Tip>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Sparrow">
    1. Open Sparrow and load your wallet.
    2. Select **Settings** in the left menu.

       <Frame>
         <img src="/images/wallet-guides/sparrow-find-xpub-step-1.png" alt="Sparrow left menu with Settings selected" />
       </Frame>

    3. Find the **Keystore** section. Your extended public key is shown there. Copy it.

       <Frame>
         <img src="/images/wallet-guides/sparrow-find-xpub-step-2.png" alt="Sparrow Keystore section showing the extended public key" />
       </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="BlueWallet">
    The steps are the same on mobile and desktop.

    1. Open BlueWallet. Select your wallet.

       <Frame>
         <img src="/images/wallet-guides/find-xpub-blue-wallet-1.png" alt="BlueWallet wallet list" />
       </Frame>

    2. Tap the **three dots** at the top right.

       <Frame>
         <img src="/images/wallet-guides/find-xpub-blue-wallet-2.png" alt="BlueWallet wallet screen with the menu button" />
       </Frame>

    3. Scroll down. Select **Export / Backup**.

       <Frame>
         <img src="/images/wallet-guides/find-xpub-blue-wallet-3.png" alt="BlueWallet wallet settings with Export / Backup" />
       </Frame>

    4. Copy the key shown at the bottom.

       <Frame>
         <img src="/images/wallet-guides/find-xpub-blue-wallet-4.png" alt="BlueWallet export screen showing the extended public key" />
       </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Blockstream (Jade)">
    1. Connect and unlock your Jade. Open the Blockstream desktop app.
    2. Select the **three dots** next to the device name.

       <Frame>
         <img src="/images/wallet-guides/blockstream-wallet-find-xpub-step-1.png" alt="Blockstream app with the device menu button" />
       </Frame>

    3. Choose **Settings**.

       <Frame>
         <img src="/images/wallet-guides/blockstream-wallet-find-xpub-step-2.png" alt="Blockstream device menu with Settings" />
       </Frame>

    4. Select **Watch-only**. Under **Extended public keys**, copy the first entry, labeled **Standard**.

       <Frame>
         <img src="/images/wallet-guides/blockstream-wallet-find-xpub-step-3.png" alt="Blockstream Watch-only settings listing extended public keys" />
       </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>An extended public key reveals all your past and future addresses for that account. Share it only with apps you trust.</Warning>
