Use **Manual Entry** for a Bitcoin amount you type, and for Bitcoin ETF shares.

<Note>
These holdings are not verified. They cannot get the Hoseki Verified badge.

To attach a hardware wallet and prove control with a signature, use [Advanced manual signing](/accounts/hardware-wallets#advanced-manual-signing). Multisig cannot use that path.
</Note>

1. Open **Accounts** → **Add Account** → **Manual Entry**.
2. Pick **Bitcoin** or **ETF**.

<Frame caption="Pick a holding type.">
  <img
    src="/images/accounts-manual-type.png"
    alt="Manual Entry type choice: Bitcoin or ETF"
  />
</Frame>

3. Enter the amount. Update it when it changes.
