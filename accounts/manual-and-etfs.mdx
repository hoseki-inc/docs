Use these for Bitcoin you cannot connect, and for Bitcoin ETF shares.

1. Open **Accounts** → **Add Account** → **Manual Entry**.
2. Pick **Bitcoin** or **ETF**.

<Frame caption="Pick a holding type.">
  <img
    src="/images/accounts-manual-type.png"
    alt="Manual Entry type choice: Bitcoin or ETF"
  />
</Frame>

3. Enter the amount. Update it when it changes.

  <Tab title="Bitcoin">
    <Frame caption="Name and amount.">

        alt="Manual Bitcoin form with name and amount"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="ETF">
    <Frame caption="ETF holdings.">

      />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
**Manual Entry** stores a balance you type. These holdings are not verified. They cannot get the Hoseki Verified badge. To attach a hardware wallet and prove control, use [Advanced](/accounts/hardware-wallets#prove-control-manually).
</Note>
