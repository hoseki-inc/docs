Use these for Bitcoin you cannot connect, and for Bitcoin ETF shares.

1. Open **Accounts** → **Add Account**.
2. Pick **Manual Entry**. Then pick **Bitcoin** or **ETF**.
3. Enter the amount. Update it when it changes.

<Note>Manual and ETF holdings are not verified. They cannot get the Hoseki Verified badge.</Note>
