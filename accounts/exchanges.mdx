Control Center connects with an **API key** you create on the exchange.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a read-only key">
    On the exchange, allow **view** only. No trading. No withdrawals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the account">
    Open **Accounts** → **Add Account** → your exchange.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste and connect">
    Paste the key fields. Select **Connect**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
