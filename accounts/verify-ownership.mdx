To put a wallet on a [statement](/statements/statements-and-hoseki-verified), you prove you control it. You do this by signing a short message with your wallet.

<Info>Signing a message **cannot move funds**. It is not a transaction. Nothing is broadcast to the Bitcoin network.</Info>

## Why verify

| | Pending wallet | Verified wallet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Balance tracked | Yes | Yes |
| Shown on Home and Portfolio | Yes | Yes |
| Can go on a statement | No | Yes |

Only want to track a wallet? You can leave it pending.

## What you sign

The message looks like this:

```text
I, the owner, affirm sole control of this account. No other third-party claims/access permitted. Ownership revocable solely by me. ID=…
```

The `ID` is unique to your account. You sign with the wallet's **first receive address** (path ending `/0/0`).

## How to sign

You sign right after adding a wallet. To finish later, open the **pending** account and select **Continue**.

</Frame>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Ledger and Trezor">
    1. Select **Prove Control**.
    2. Check the message and address on your device. Approve it.

    <Frame caption="Sign on your device.">
      <img
        src="/images/statements-signing.png"
        alt="Hardware wallet signing prompt for proof of control"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="COLDCARD (MicroSD)">
    1. Select **Download** to save `hoseki-ownership-claim.txt`. Copy it to your MicroSD card. Do not edit it.
    2. On the COLDCARD, go to **Advanced/Tools** → **File Management** → **Sign Text File**. Pick the file. Press Enter.
    3. Upload the new `hoseki-ownership-claim-signed.txt`. Select **Prove Control**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="COLDCARD Q and Keystone">
    1. Scan the QR code on screen with your device.
    2. Confirm and sign on the device.
    3. Select **Ready to Scan**. Hold the signed QR up to your webcam.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="xpub or descriptor">
    1. Copy the **Address** and **Message**.
    2. Sign it in your wallet software (Sparrow, Electrum, Bitcoin Core, or similar).
    3. Paste the signature. Select **Verify**.

    <Frame caption="Sign with any wallet tool and paste the result.">
      <img

        alt="Manual signing screen with Address, Message, and Signature fields"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

When it works, you see **Claim Verified!** Choose **Generate Statement**, **Connect Another Account**, or **Back to Home**.

## Good to know

- **Verification does not expire.** You sign once per wallet.
- **The message says "the owner".** It does not include your name. Your name on statements comes from [Settings](/settings/app-settings).
- **Multisig** needs a signature from enough keys to meet your quorum. See [Multisig](/accounts/multisig).
- **Stuck waiting for the device?** After 20 seconds, a link appears to reset the step.

## If signing fails

| Message | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| **Invalid signature** | Sign the exact message shown, with the exact address shown. Don't add spaces or line breaks. |
| **Signing address does not match the account key** | You signed with a different address or wallet. Use the address shown. |
| **File not downloaded or copied** | Download or copy the message file before you select **Next**. |
| **The signing process did not complete successfully** (COLDCARD) | Upload the file ending `-signed.txt`, not the original. |
