Control Center imports **public** keys (xpubs) only. Your coins stay on your wallet.

## Supported

| Wallet | How it connects |
| --- | --- |
| Ledger | USB |
| Trezor | USB |
| Coldcard | MicroSD file or QR code |
| Keystone | QR code |
| Multisig | Combine supported wallets |

## USB tips

- Use a Chromium browser (Chrome, Brave, Edge) on StartOS or Umbrel.
- Close other wallet apps first.

<Warning>Never type your seed phrase into Control Center. Approve signatures on the device only.</Warning>
