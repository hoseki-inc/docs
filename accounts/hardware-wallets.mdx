Control Center imports **public** keys (xpubs) only. Your coins stay on your wallet.

## Supported

| Wallet | How it connects |
| --- | --- |
| Ledger | USB |
| Trezor | USB |
| Coldcard | MicroSD file or QR code |
| Keystone | QR code |
| Multisig | Descriptor, then sign on each device |

  <Tab title="Advanced" id="advanced">
    Use this to attach a single-signature hardware wallet and prove you control it. You sign a message on the device, then paste the signature.

    **Manual Entry** stores a balance you type. **Advanced** checks a signature. See [Manual Bitcoin and ETFs](/accounts/manual-and-etfs).

    1. Open **Accounts** → **Add Account**.
    2. Under the account grid, open **Advanced** (**Manually Verify Your Self-Custodial Bitcoin Holdings**).
    3. Select **Verify with Manual Signing**.
    4. Enter a **Display name**.
    5. Paste an **Account descriptor or extended public key**.
    6. Select **Add Account**.
    7. Sign the **Message** for the **Address** on your hardware wallet.
    8. Paste the **Signature**.
    9. Select **Verify**.

    The **Address** is the first receive address. Prefer an account descriptor. It includes the script type. If the key starts with `x`, choose **Select script type**: Legacy (P2PKH), Nested SegWit (P2SH), Native SegWit (P2WPKH), or Taproot (P2TR).

    <Note>Your seed stays on the device. Paste the public account data and the signature only.</Note>

    Multisig cannot use this path. See [Multisig](#multisig).

## USB tips

- Use a Chromium browser (Chrome, Brave, Edge) on StartOS or Umbrel.
- Close other wallet apps first.

## Multisig

Open **Accounts** → **Add Account** → **Multisig**.

Export the descriptor from your hardware wallet or a coordinator such as Sparrow, Unchained, or Nunchuk. Upload the file. Or open **Paste Your Multisig Descriptor**, paste it, and select **Verify Descriptor**.

Enter a **Name**. Select **Next**.

Sign on each required device. Choose **Select a Signing Device**: Trezor, Ledger, Coldcard, or Keystone. You can sign the keys in any order.

A 2-of-3 wallet needs two signatures. A wallet that requires every key needs all of them.

<Warning>
**Advanced** does not work for multisig. Each required key signs its own message on the device. There is no single pasteable BIP322-style proof for the multisig script. This path needs message signing through your wallet. Many wallets do not support the message signing needed for this prove-control path (BIP322).
</Warning>

<Warning>Never type your seed phrase into Control Center. Approve signatures on the device only.</Warning>
