<Steps>
  <Step title="Install">
    Pick [desktop](/getting-started/install-desktop), [StartOS](/getting-started/install-startos), or [Umbrel](/getting-started/install-umbrel).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to Electrum">
    Choose the Electrum server for balances. See [First run](/getting-started/first-run).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an account">
    Open **Accounts** → **Add Account**. Pick a [wallet](/accounts/hardware-wallets), [exchange](/accounts/exchanges), or [manual](/accounts/manual-and-etfs) holding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for sync">
    New wallets take a few minutes. Large wallets take longer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back up">
    Set up [backups](/maintenance/backup-restore) before you rely on it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never enter a seed phrase or private key. Control Center never asks for one.
</Warning>
