<Steps>
  <Step title="Install">
    Pick [desktop](/getting-started/install-desktop), [StartOS](/getting-started/install-startos), or [Umbrel](/getting-started/install-umbrel).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your PIN">
    Choose a 6-digit PIN. You enter it each time you open Control Center. See [PIN and lock](/security/pin).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an Electrum server">
    On desktop, the app starts on a public server. Switch to your own for privacy. See [First run](/getting-started/first-run).

    StartOS and Umbrel use your own node automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an account">
    Select **Add Account**. Pick a [wallet](/accounts/hardware-wallets), [exchange](/accounts/exchanges), or [manual](/accounts/manual-and-etfs) holding.

    <Frame caption="Pick an account type.">
      <img
        src="/images/accounts-add-account.png"
        alt="Add Account screen listing wallets, exchanges, and manual entry"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prove control (wallets only)">
    Sign one message on your hardware wallet. This marks the wallet **verified** so it can go on a [statement](/statements/statements-and-hoseki-verified). See [Verify ownership](/accounts/verify-ownership).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back up">
    Set up [backups](/maintenance/backup-restore) before you rely on it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never enter a seed phrase or private key. Control Center never asks for one.
</Warning>
