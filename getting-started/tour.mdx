The menu runs across the top of every page.

| Item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your total, the Bitcoin price, and recent statements. |
| **Accounts** | Add, open, rename, and archive accounts. |
| **Portfolio** | Balance history and changes over time, by account. |
| **Statements** | Create and download statements. |
| Gear icon | [Settings](/settings/app-settings). |
| Padlock icon | Lock the app. Appears when a PIN is in use. |

## Home

Your total in BTC and USD, your 30-day change, and the Bitcoin price.

<Frame caption="Home with two accounts.">
  <img
    src="/images/tour-home.png"
    alt="Home page showing total balance, 30-day chart, 30d change, accounts count, Bitcoin price chart, and statements banner"
  />
</Frame>

- **30d Change:** your total today minus your total 30 days ago.
- **Bitcoin Price:** USD only. Choose **7D**, **30D**, **6M**, or **1Y**.
- **Recent Statements:** your last three statements.

Before you add accounts, Home shows a zero balance and an **Add Account** button.
