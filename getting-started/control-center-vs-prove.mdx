| | Bitcoin Control Center | Prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Runs | On your device or node | Hosted by Hoseki |
| Account | None | Hoseki account |
| Your data | Stays local | Stored by Hoseki |
| Cost | Free, open source | Paid credits |
| Help | This site | [help.hoseki.app](https://help.hoseki.app) |

## How to tell which one you use

- You installed an app, or a StartOS or Umbrel service: **Control Center**.
- You sign in at hoseki.app: **Prove**.

Prove articles do not apply to Control Center. Example: Control Center never sends your xpub to Hoseki.
