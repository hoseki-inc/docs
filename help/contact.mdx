- **Email:** [support@bitcoincontrolcenter.com](mailto:support@bitcoincontrolcenter.com)
- **Bugs and feature requests:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/hoseki-inc/bitcoin-control-center/issues)
- **Security issues:** report privately. See [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/hoseki-inc/bitcoin-control-center/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

Include your platform, app version, and what you tried.

<Warning>Never share your seed phrase, private keys, API keys, or xpubs. Support will never ask.</Warning>
