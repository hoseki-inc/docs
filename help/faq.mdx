<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is it free?">
    Yes. It is open source under Apache 2.0.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it spend my Bitcoin?">
    No. It is watch-only. It never has your private keys.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Hoseki account?">
    No.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Hoseki see my data?">
    No. Your data stays on your device.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need my own node?">
    No. You need an Electrum server. Your own is best for privacy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it work on Intel Macs or Linux desktops?">
    Not yet. Use an Apple Silicon Mac, Windows x64, StartOS, or Umbrel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which wallets and exchanges work?">
    Ledger, Trezor, Coldcard, Keystone, and multisig. Coinbase, Kraken, Robinhood, and Strike. Plus typed manual balances and ETFs.

    Single-signature wallets can also use [Advanced manual signing](/accounts/hardware-wallets#advanced-manual-signing). Multisig cannot. Many wallets do not support BIP322 yet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I lose my computer?">
    Your coins are safe. Restore from a [backup](/maintenance/backup-restore), or re-add your accounts.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
