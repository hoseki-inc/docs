A statement is a PDF of your verified claims for a date range. It shows opening balance, money in, money out, and closing balance for each claim.

## Before you start

- You need at least one **verified** claim. Pending claims aren't listed.
- Each statement you create uses **1 credit**. You're charged only when it finishes. Statements for a partner's [request](/prove/requests-and-sharing) are free to you.

## Create a statement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Select **Generate Statement** on **Home** or **Statements**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick dates">
    Choose a **Start Date** and **End Date**. The default is the last 3 months. Dates use your browser's time zone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add contact details (optional)">
    Open **Contact Information**. Add a **Name** and **Address**. They print exactly as you type them. Your email always prints.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick claims">
    Check the claims to include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    Select **Preview Statement** to check it. Previews are free.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Select **Generate Statement**. When it's ready, select **View Statement**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Choose **Full Period** or **Monthly**. Select **Download**. Downloading again is free.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Date rules

- Start no earlier than April 30, 2013.
- End no later than today.
- Start must be on or before end.

## What's on the PDF

- Your email, plus your name and address if you added them.
- A summary per claim: opening balance, money out, money in, and closing balance. Claims are listed as **Account #1**, **Account #2**, and so on.
- A page of transactions for each claim. Unconfirmed transactions show "PENDING".
- Your total in BTC.
- The **Hoseki Verified** badge.

The PDF does not include your wallet addresses, transaction IDs, or public keys.

## Hoseki Verified

The badge means each claim on the statement passed proof of control in Prove. You signed a message with that wallet's key. Balances and transactions come from the Bitcoin blockchain. Prices come from CoinGecko.

## Find past statements

**Statements** lists everything you've made, plus statements made for partner requests. The **Requester** column shows "Self-initiated" or the partner's name.

## Out of credits?

You'll see "You need 1 credit to generate this statement." Select **⚡️ Buy 1 Credit Now** or **See Bundles**. See [Credits](/prove/credits).
