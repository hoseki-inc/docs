## Before you start

- **A computer.** Prove works on desktop browsers. Phones and tablets show **Desktop Required**.
- **Chrome, Brave, or Edge** if you use a Ledger.
- **Your hardware wallet**, or a wallet app that can sign messages.

## Sign in or create an account

Prove has no password. You sign in with a code sent to your email.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Go to [prove.hoseki.app](https://prove.hoseki.app). Enter your email. Select **Login**, or **Create Account** if you're new. Complete the security check.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    Check your email for **Login for Hoseki**. Enter the 6-digit code. It works for 5 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    New accounts start with 5 free credits. Each statement you create uses 1 credit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>Hoseki will never ask for your passcode. Don't share it with anyone.</Warning>

**No code?** Check spam. Wait for the timer, then select **Resend code**. You can request a new code once a minute.

## Find your way around

| Item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Pending requests, recent statements, and recent requests. |
| **Claims** | Connect, verify, and manage your wallets. |
| **Statements** | Create, preview, and download statements. |
| **Requests** | Statement and sharing requests from partners. |
| **Sharing** | Partners you share claims with. Stop sharing here. |
| Credit balance | Buy credits and see your purchase history. |
| Help icon | Chat with support. |
| Your email | **Sign Out**. |

## Staying signed in

You stay signed in for up to 7 days without activity. **Sign Out** signs you out on every device.
