Open a claim from **Claims** to see its balance, status, script type, and date added.

## Rename a claim

Select **Rename**. Enter a name. Select **Save**.

<Note>Partners you share a claim with can see its name. Statements show the system name, such as **Account #1**, not your custom name.</Note>

## Finish a pending claim

Open the claim. Select **Continue** to sign the message.

## Delete a claim

| Status | How to delete |
| --- | --- |
| **pending** | Select **Delete Claim** → **Delete**. |
| **verified** | Email [support@hoseki.app](mailto:support@hoseki.app) with the **Claim ID** shown in the delete dialog. |

## See who a claim is shared with

If you share a claim, its page shows **Sharing Authorizations**. Select **Edit** to manage it. See [Requests and sharing](/prove/requests-and-sharing).

## Close your account

Email [support@hoseki.app](mailto:support@hoseki.app) from your account's email. Support deletes your claims, statements, and credit history. Partners keep any statements and data you already shared with them.
