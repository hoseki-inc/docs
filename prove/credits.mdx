Each statement you create uses **1 credit**. New accounts get **5 free credits**. Credits never expire.

<Note>Don't see a credit balance at the top of Prove? Then statements are free on your account.</Note>

## What uses a credit

| Action | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Create a statement | 1, charged when it finishes |
| Statement that fails | 0 |
| Preview a statement | 0 |
| Download a statement again | 0 |
| Complete a partner's request | 0 |
| Add or verify a claim | 0 |

## Buy credits

Select your credit balance at the top of the page, or **Manage Credits** in your account menu.

| Bundle | Price | Pay with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 credit | $2.99 | Lightning |
| 5 credits | $11.99 | Card |
| 10 credits | $19.99 | Card |

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Lightning">
    1. Select the 1-credit bundle. Choose **Lightning Network**. Select **Generate Invoice**.
    2. Scan the QR code with a Lightning wallet, or select **Copy Invoice**.
    3. Pay before the timer runs out. If it expires, select **Refresh**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Card">
    1. Select a 5- or 10-credit bundle. Choose **Credit Card**. Select **Continue**.
    2. Select **Pay Now**. Pay on the Stripe page.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

You see **Payment Complete!** and get a receipt by email: **Credit Purchase Receipt for Hoseki**.

## Purchase history

Open your account menu → **Purchase History**. It lists purchases, credits used, and bonus credits. Select **View** for a receipt.
