Bitcoin Control Center tracks your Bitcoin savings on your own device. It is watch-only. It never holds your seed or private keys. There is no Hoseki account.

## How it works

Control Center runs on your computer or home server. It reads your balances from the sources you choose and keeps everything it learns on your device.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  subgraph You["Your device"]
    CC["Bitcoin Control Center<br/>accounts, history, statements"]
  end
  E["Electrum server<br/>wallet balances"] --> CC
  X["Exchanges<br/>read-only API keys"] --> CC
  P["Price source<br/>Bitcoin price"] --> CC
  M["You<br/>manual and ETF holdings"] --> CC
```

- **Wallets** are read from an Electrum server, using public keys only.
- **Exchanges** are read with a read-only API key you create.
- **Manual and ETF holdings** are numbers you type in.
- **Prices** come from a price source you pick in [Settings](/settings/app-settings).

Nothing is sent to Hoseki. See [Privacy and security](/privacy-security) for exactly what leaves your device.
