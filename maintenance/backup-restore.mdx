Your data lives only on your device. Hoseki cannot recover it.

## Desktop

1. Quit the app fully.
2. Copy the whole data folder.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mac">
    `~/Library/Application Support/desktop/data`

    <Frame caption="The data folder on Mac. Screenshot coming soon.">
      <img
        src="/images/backup-mac-folder.png"
        alt="Finder showing the Control Center data folder on Mac"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Windows">
    `%APPDATA%\desktop\data`

    <Frame caption="The data folder on Windows. Screenshot coming soon.">
      <img
        src="/images/backup-windows-folder.png"
        alt="File Explorer showing the Control Center data folder on Windows"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

3. To restore, quit the app. Put the folder back. Reopen.

<Warning>Copy the whole folder. The database and the `secrets` key must stay together. Without the key, saved exchange keys cannot be read.</Warning>

## StartOS

Use the normal StartOS backup. It saves everything.

## After a restore

Check accounts and history. If an exchange asks, reconnect it.

<Frame caption="Reconnect an exchange. Screenshot coming soon.">
  <img
    src="/images/backup-exchange-reconnect.png"
    alt="Exchange account reconnect prompt after restore"
  />
</Frame>

Lost your data? Re-add your wallets. Your coins are never at risk. Only history and settings are lost.
